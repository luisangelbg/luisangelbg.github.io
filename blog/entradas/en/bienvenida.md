This site brings together in one place the scientific applications I have developed for research and teaching in the agricultural and biological sciences.

## What they have in common

Every application in the suite shares three principles:

- **They run in your browser.** There is nothing to install and no account to create. It is enough to open the link in an up-to-date desktop browser.
- **Your data never leaves your computer.** The computation happens in the browser itself; there is no server that receives your files.
- **They are free software.** They are distributed under the GPL licence and their source code is on GitHub, so anyone can check how each result is computed.

## How the site is organised

- **Applications** holds the full catalogue, grouped by field: statistics, agricultural experimentation, genetics, biogeography, botany and tools for science.
- **Downloads** holds the user manuals in PDF, the example data and the source code of each application so you can use it offline.
- **Publications** holds the laboratory's papers and chapters, with their DOIs.
- Here, in the **blog**, I will post news, how-to guides and the materials of the workshops.

Some applications are shown as *Coming soon*: they are finished or in their last round of testing and will open over the coming weeks.
