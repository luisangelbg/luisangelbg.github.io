This workshop of roughly **six hours** works on species distribution modelling with an emphasis on the science and on data analysis. The aim is that each participant poses a research question and answers it with real data, using the applications of the suite.

## What we will do

1. **Pose the question.** Which species, which region and what we want to know about its distribution.
2. **Obtain and clean the presence records** from open biodiversity portals with BioModelling Pro.
3. **Explore the variables**, bioclimatic and soil; reduce collinearity with PCAPro and ClusteringPro.
4. **Model and validate** the distribution with several algorithms and an ensemble.
5. **Interpret and communicate**: maps, thresholds, variable importance and the methods text.

## What you need

- A computer with an up-to-date desktop browser and an internet connection (to download the records and the base maps).
- The global climate layers downloaded beforehand; the BioModelling Pro manual explains which ones and where to get them.
- Nothing else: there are no programs to install.
